# Official effects observation

Source page: https://pikpok.com/games/super-monsters-ate-my-condo/
Official press-kit video: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nDrTxQ8i0chLXDjUq6ruHcB2o2dmnEsg/view
YouTube launch trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6odGPop0ib0 (metadata available; video download returned 403, so no timing claims are made from this version).

Observed the press-kit video around 4.9–5.5 s and 8.0–9.5 s using frame contact sheets at 20 fps. Durations below are approximate visual estimates from a promotional edit, not extracted game constants.

- A broad horizontal translucent color band crosses the entire playfield at the match height. Normal matches use the matched color (green / blue); higher combos use bright yellow-gold.
- Matching floors briefly become pale / luminous. The stack remains readable for roughly 0.25–0.45 s before merging; floors above descend, rebound, and sway.
- COMBO and score text pop in at a slight angle over the floors. High combos accumulate EPIC / ULTRA / SUPER / COMBO vertically, plus a side ×2 CHAIN label.
- Score numbers then rise toward the upper left along thin bright streaks, with a white starburst at the leading end.
- Gold combo light becomes a vertical bright column over the middle of the tower. Small multicolor confetti pieces scatter rather than obscuring the tower with dense smoke.
- Power state has a moving horizontal chain of bright cyan/yellow/magenta chevrons at floor height and flickering patterned special condos.
- New floors fall from above individually while lower merges resolve, preserving continuous pressure.
- Monsters are large side spectators with reactive mouths and body motion; condo floors remain clearly distinguishable at screen center.

Art inventory: the official kit contains screenshots, app icon, full key art, logo, and video. No individual transparent monster or scenery layers were present. The downloaded logo is RGBA 4000×4000 with large transparent margins; the key art is RGB 1920×1080; the screenshot is RGB 1080×1920. Do not treat the key art as an isolated character sprite.
